Frequently Asked Questions

What types of weighing scales does Prime Systems offer?

Prime Systems offers weighing solutions for commercial and industrial applications. Available types and configurations depend on the product range and specific requirements.

How do I choose the right weighing scale?

Consider the maximum load, required readability, platform dimensions, operating environment and intended application. Contact Prime Systems to discuss suitable available models.

What weighing capacities are available?

Weighing capacity varies by model and scale type. Share your expected load requirements with Prime Systems to enquire about suitable capacity options.

Are digital weighing scales suitable for industrial use?

Yes, suitable digital weighing scales can be used in industrial environments. The appropriate model depends on load requirements, operating conditions and the intended weighing application.

What is the difference between a weighing scale and an industrial platform scale?

General weighing scales are available in different designs for various weighing tasks, while industrial platform scales are designed for weighing larger or heavier loads, depending on their rated capacity and platform size.

How can I maintain the accuracy of my weighing scale?

Place the scale on a suitable, stable surface, avoid overloading it, keep the weighing platform clean and follow the manufacturer's operating instructions. Arrange calibration or servicing when required.

Does Prime Systems provide weighing scale servicing and calibration?

Service and calibration availability should be confirmed directly with Prime Systems. Contact the team to discuss your equipment and the support you require.
