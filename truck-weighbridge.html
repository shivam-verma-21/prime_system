Types of Weighbridges

Choose a weighbridge arrangement based on site layout, installation conditions and vehicle movement requirements.

Pit Type Weighbridge

A pit type weighbridge is installed in a foundation pit so its weighing platform sits level with the surrounding ground. With no raised ramps at either end, it can be a practical option for sites where space is limited or vehicles need to approach the platform from different directions. This configuration is commonly considered for public weighbridges and busy vehicle-entry areas.

Before choosing this design, check whether excavation and pit construction are suitable for the site. Ground conditions, water drainage, available approach space and access for inspection and cleaning should all be considered during planning. The right arrangement depends on the vehicles being weighed and the layout of your facility.

Benefits
  • Uses less surface space because long approach ramps are not required.
  • The level platform can support convenient access for trucks and other suitable vehicles, subject to the selected model and site layout.
  • Vehicles can approach from multiple directions where the surrounding site layout allows.
  • Its flush installation can suit locations with frequent vehicle movement and limited room for ramps.

Pitless Type Weighbridge

A pitless weighbridge is installed above ground level, making it an option for sites where excavating a foundation pit is difficult, unsuitable or costly. Vehicles drive onto the raised weighing platform using access ramps, so the site needs enough clear space for the ramps and safe vehicle movement.

Because there is no deep pit beneath the platform, this arrangement can help avoid water collecting in a weighbridge pit and makes key components easier to reach for inspection. When planning an installation, consider the available approach area, ground and drainage conditions, vehicle sizes, and the space needed around the structure for routine checks. Suitability depends on the selected model and site layout.

Benefits
  • Avoids excavation and construction of a weighbridge pit.
  • Raised installation can help prevent waterlogging in a pit during rainy weather.
  • Removes the need for routine cleaning and upkeep inside a weighbridge pit.
  • Above-ground components can be easier to access for inspection and servicing.
  • Access ramps provide a defined route onto the platform when the site has sufficient space.

Mobile Weighbridge

Mobile weighbridges are designed for sites that need a relocatable weighing solution. The modular unit rests on a suitable smooth, hard surface, reducing the need for conventional foundation work. Its modular construction can make transportation and re-installation more convenient.

These systems may be supplied with a steel-fabricated bottom frame, top platform and ramps. Load cells are installed between the platform and supporting frame. Actual installation time and site preparation depend on the selected model and site conditions.

Benefits
  • Modular design may include platform, bottom frame and ramps.
  • Can reduce conventional foundation work where site conditions permit.
  • Designed for comparatively quick installation after site preparation.
  • Can be relocated to another suitable site.
  • Useful for temporary or changing project locations.
  • May help reduce construction and relocation costs.
Features
  • Steel-fabricated modular platform construction.
  • Anti-skid, ribbed top plate on applicable models.
  • Bottom frame with supporting joists beneath the platform.
  • Ramp configuration and protection ratings depend on the selected model.
Applications
  • Forestry and landfill sites
  • Roading and construction materials
  • Demolition material sites
  • Agriculture and quarries
  • Military and temporary project sites
  • Construction industry

Frequently Asked Questions

What is a truck weighbridge?

A truck weighbridge is a heavy-duty weighing system designed to measure the weight of trucks and other commercial vehicles. It is commonly used in industries, logistics, manufacturing, agriculture and material transportation.

What is the difference between pit type and pitless type weighbridges?

A pit type weighbridge is installed flush with ground level and generally does not require access ramps. A pitless weighbridge is installed above ground level and typically requires ramps at both ends for vehicle access.

What are the benefits of a pit type weighbridge?

Pit type weighbridges require less surface space because access ramps are not needed. Their ground-level platform can accommodate vehicles approaching from different directions, depending on the site layout.

When should I choose a pitless weighbridge?

A pitless weighbridge can be suitable when excavation is difficult or pit construction is expensive. Its above-ground design provides easier access to components for maintenance and helps avoid water accumulation inside a weighing pit.

What is a mobile weighbridge?

A mobile weighbridge is a modular weighing system designed for relocation between suitable sites. Depending on its configuration, it can be installed on a properly prepared, hard and level surface, reducing the need for conventional permanent foundations.

What are the main benefits of a mobile weighbridge?

Mobile weighbridges offer flexibility, easier relocation and potentially reduced installation time. Their modular construction can also help reduce foundation and transportation costs, depending on the model and site.

What is a rail in-motion weighbridge?

A rail in-motion weighbridge is a railway weighing system designed to weigh railway wagons while they move over a specially configured weighing track. It is used in railway freight operations and industrial material transportation.

Which industries use truck weighbridges?

Truck weighbridges are used in manufacturing, logistics, warehousing, construction, agriculture, mining, quarries, waste management and other industries that need to measure vehicle weight or transported materials.

How do I choose the right weighbridge for my site?

Consider your vehicle types, required weighing capacity, available space, site conditions, installation budget and maintenance requirements. These factors help determine the most suitable weighbridge configuration for your needs.

What weighing capacity is available for truck weighbridges?

Weighing capacity varies according to the weighbridge model and configuration. Contact Prime Systems with your vehicle type and expected maximum load to enquire about suitable capacity options.
