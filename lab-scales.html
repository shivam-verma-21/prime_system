Frequently Asked Questions

What is an laboratory scale used for?

An laboratory scale is used to weigh materials, components, containers, cartons and other goods placed on its platform. The suitable model depends on load capacity, weighing capacity and readability and the working environment.

How do I choose the right laboratory scale?

Consider the maximum load, weighing capacity and readability, required readability, installation space and operating conditions. Contact Prime Systems to discuss suitable available models for your application in Umbergaon.

What capacities and pan sizes are available?

Rated capacity and weighing capacity and readability vary by model. Share the size and weight of the items you need to weigh so the team can discuss suitable available options.

Can laboratory scales be used in manufacturing and warehouses?

Yes. A suitably rated laboratory scale can support weighing tasks in manufacturing, warehousing, stock handling and dispatch. Select a model according to the load, pan size and operating environment.

What is the difference between a tabletop scale and a laboratory scale?

Tabletop scales are generally used for smaller items and compact workspaces. Industrial laboratory scales provide a larger load-bearing surface for heavier or bulkier goods, subject to the selected model’s rated capacity.

How can I maintain an laboratory scale?

Install the scale on a stable, suitable surface, keep the platform clean, avoid overloading and follow the manufacturer’s operating instructions. Arrange inspection or calibration when required.

Does Prime Systems provide laboratory scale servicing or calibration?

Please confirm service and calibration availability directly with Prime Systems. Contact the team to discuss your equipment and the support you require.
