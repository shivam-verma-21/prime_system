What weighing products does Prime Systems offer?

Prime Systems offers weighing equipment for commercial, industrial and laboratory applications, including weighing scales, industrial platform scales, laboratory scales and truck weighbridges.

How do I choose the right weighing scale for my business?

The right weighing scale depends on your application, required weighing capacity, accuracy and operating environment. Contact Prime Systems to discuss your specific weighing requirements.

What is the difference between a platform scale and a regular weighing scale?

Platform scales are generally designed for weighing larger or heavier loads, making them suitable for industrial and commercial applications. Regular weighing scales are available for various everyday weighing requirements.

Where are laboratory weighing scales used?

Laboratory weighing scales are used for precision measurement in laboratories, testing facilities and other applications where accurate weighing is important. Suitable equipment depends on the required precision and intended use.

What is a truck weighbridge used for?

A truck weighbridge is used to measure the weight of trucks and other heavy vehicles. It is commonly used in industrial facilities, warehouses, logistics operations and transportation businesses.
